Beauty Is in the Eye of the Beholder

Can a machine learning model tell whether two people are meant to be together? Upload a group photo and see which two people it picks.

The app finds every face, compares every possible pair on how alike they are and how they’re posed, and shows you the single most likely match.

      How the match works

      What it reads

      • Estimated age and apparent gender
      • Height in the photo and outfit colour
      • How close people stand and whether they lean in

      How it decides

      Every possible man–woman pair gets a score out of 100 for how alike the two people are and how they’re posed. The best pairs are picked without using anyone twice, so a photo with two couples gives two pairs. It’s an experiment, not proof: friends pose close together too.

      Safeguards and privacy

      • Skin colour is never used
      • Anyone who may be under 18 is never matched, and nor is anyone whose face is too small to check
      • Nobody is identified, and your photo isn’t saved. Only the measurements are logged on this computer, to help fix mistakes.

      The Dating Game is an academic machine learning project. Face detection uses YuNet from the OpenCV Model Zoo; age and expression models come from the ONNX Model Zoo.
